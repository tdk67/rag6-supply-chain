    BSI C5:2024 Sovereign Cloud Security & Attestation Report
    Document ID: AUD-BSI-C5-2024-01 | Attestation Release: 2024.1 | Audit Date: 2025-01-10
    Auditing Authority: TÜV Informationstechnik GmbH & German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) | Target Scope: Aethelgard Sovereign AI Facility DC-1 Frankfurt
    

    Section 1: Executive Compliance Attestation
    Aethelgard DC-1 Frankfurt am Main has passed all 17 criteria domains of the BSI Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue (C5:2024), certifying strict sovereign data residency and physical operational autonomy within Germany.

    Section 2: European Data Residency & Hardware Cryptography
    2.1 Data Sovereignty: All customer tenant weights, telemetry data, and training checkpoints are guaranteed zero extraterritorial transmission. All physical storage drives (NVMe E1.S) remain strictly within European Union territorial boundaries.
    2.2 Hardware Security Modules (HSM): Cryptographic root keys, tenant isolation keys, and TLS termination certificates are strictly managed by European-manufactured Sovereign Hardware Security Modules (Eviden Trustway Sovereign HSM, model SKU-SEC-HSM-SOV) certified under Common Criteria EAL4+ and BSI C5:2024 criteria.

    Section 3: EU AI Act Conformity Criteria
    3.1 Compliant with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act) Article 10 (Data and Data Governance) and Article 15 (Cybersecurity and Robustness).
    3.2 High-risk AI systems deployed on Aethelgard compute clusters maintain continuous audit logs, automated SBOM vulnerability scanning, and isolated confidential compute domains.

    Section 4: Supply Chain Security & NIS2 Directive
    4.1 Full adherence to Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) Chapter IV regarding supply chain cybersecurity risk management for essential and important entities.
    4.2 Continuous vetting and multi-tier cryptographic pedigree verification for all Tier-1 and Tier-2 suppliers.
